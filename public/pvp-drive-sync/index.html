pvp-drive-sync

pvp-drive-sync is a private tool used only by its developer, brainage04. It reads Minecraft PvP recordings that people upload through the "Minecraft PvP recordings upload" Google Form into the developer's own Google Drive folder, and copies them to the developer's computer to train bots for a private Minecraft server.

It asks for read-only access to Google Drive, and only the developer's own account signs in to it.
