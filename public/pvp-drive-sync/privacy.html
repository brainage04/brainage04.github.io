pvp-drive-sync privacy policy

pvp-drive-sync is a private tool. Only its developer, brainage04, signs in to it, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With read-only Google Drive access, it reads the files in the developer's own Drive folder that receives uploads from the "Minecraft PvP recordings upload" Google Form, and copies them to the developer's computer. It does not change or delete anything in Google Drive, and it does not read any other account's data.

How the recordings are used

The recordings are used only to train bots for a private Minecraft server. Player names and uploader names are replaced with anonymous ids in the processed data; the mapping back to names stays private on the developer's computer and is never shared. The data is not sold or given to anyone else.

Removal

To have your uploads and the data made from them deleted, email thomasmcmah21@gmail.com.
